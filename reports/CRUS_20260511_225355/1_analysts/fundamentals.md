# Fundamental Analysis Report: Cirrus Logic, Inc. (CRUS)
**Date of Analysis:** May 11, 2026
**Sector:** Technology | **Industry:** Semiconductors

---

## 1. Company Profile
Cirrus Logic, Inc. (CRUS) is a leading designer and manufacturer of high-performance, low-power analog and mixed-signal integrated circuits. Operating within the semiconductor industry, the company primarily focuses on audio solutions for mobile devices, computers, and other consumer electronics. With a strong market position in the high-end smartphone audio market, CRUS is a critical supplier for major global electronics brands.

## 2. Financial Performance Analysis

### Income Statement Trends
Cirrus Logic has demonstrated significant growth and profitability throughout the 2025 fiscal year.
*   **Revenue Growth:** There is a clear upward trend in quarterly revenue. After a dip in mid-2025 (Q2: $407M), revenue surged to $561M in Q3 and $581M in Q4 2025. This indicates strong seasonal demand or successful new product launches toward the end of the year.
*   **Profitability:**
    *   **Gross Profit:** Remained robust, reaching $308M in Q4 2025.
    *   **Net Income:** Net income saw a substantial increase in the second half of 2025, with Q4 reaching $140.3M compared to $60.7M in Q2.
    *   **Margins:** The company maintains a healthy profit margin of approximately 20.7% (TTM), reflecting efficient cost management and pricing power in its niche.

### Balance Sheet Strength
The company's balance sheet is exceptionally strong, characterized by high liquidity and low leverage.
*   **Liquidity:** With a **Current Ratio of 7.365**, CRUS possesses an overwhelming ability to cover its short-term obligations. Cash and cash equivalents grew to $778M by the end of 2025.
*   **Solvency:** Total debt is very low at $137.3M relative to Stockholders' Equity of $2.1B. The actual debt-to-equity ratio is approximately **0.065**, indicating minimal financial risk from leverage.
*   **Asset Base:** Total assets stand at $2.46B, with a significant portion held in liquid financial assets and cash, providing a massive cushion for R&D or strategic acquisitions.

### Cash Flow Dynamics
CRUS is a cash-generating machine with a disciplined approach to capital allocation.
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** Strong and positive, peaking at $290.8M in Q4 2025.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** FCF is consistently positive, with a notable spike to $285.7M in Q4 2025.
*   **Shareholder Returns:** The company is aggressively returning value to shareholders through share repurchases. It consistently spent roughly $100M per quarter on buybacks throughout 2025, which reduces share count and boosts EPS.

## 4. Actionable Insights for Traders

### Bullish Indicators (Buy/Hold)
*   **Accelerating Revenue:** The sharp increase in revenue and net income in H2 2025 suggests a strong growth trajectory entering 2026.
*   **Fortress Balance Sheet:** Extremely low debt and high cash reserves make the company resilient to economic downturns and give it the agility to invest in new technologies.
*   **Aggressive Buybacks:** Continuous share repurchases provide a floor for the stock price and increase the value of remaining shares.
*   **Operating Efficiency:** High ROE (20.3%) and operating margins (~20%) indicate a highly efficient business model.

### Bearish Risks (Watch)
*   **Concentration Risk:** As a semiconductor firm focused on mobile audio, CRUS is heavily dependent on a few large customers (e.g., Apple). Any shift in customer design wins could impact revenue.
*   **PEG Ratio:** A PEG ratio of 9.35 is quite high, suggesting that the stock may be overvalued relative to its immediate growth rate.
