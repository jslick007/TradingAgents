# Fundamental Analysis Report: NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA)
**Date of Analysis:** May 11, 2026

## 1. Company Profile
NVIDIA Corporation is a global leader in the **Technology** sector, specifically within the **Semiconductors** industry. The company has evolved from a graphics processing unit (GPU) manufacturer to a full-stack computing company, dominating the AI hardware market. With a massive market capitalization of approximately **$5.33 trillion**, NVIDIA is one of the most valuable companies globally, serving as the primary infrastructure provider for the generative AI revolution.

---

## 2. Financial Performance Analysis

### Revenue and Profitability
NVIDIA has demonstrated explosive growth over the last five quarters, reflecting the surging demand for AI accelerators.
*   **Revenue Trend:** Quarterly revenue has grown consistently from **$39.33 billion** (Jan 2025) to **$68.13 billion** (Jan 2026), representing a substantial growth trajectory.
*   **Net Income:** Net income has scaled rapidly, reaching **$42.96 billion** in the most recent quarter (Jan 2026), compared to $22.09 billion a year prior.
*   **Profitability Margins:**
    *   **Profit Margin:** 55.6%
    *   **Operating Margin:** 65.0%
    *   **Gross Profit Margin:** Extremely high, with the most recent quarter showing a gross profit of $51.09 billion on $68.13 billion in revenue (~74.9%).

### Earnings Per Share (EPS)
*   **TTM EPS:** $4.89
*   **Forward EPS:** $11.29
*   **Growth:** Diluted EPS has risen from $0.89 to $1.76 per quarter over the last year, indicating a strong capacity to translate revenue growth into shareholder value.

---

## 3. Financial Health and Stability

### Balance Sheet Strength
*   **Liquidity:** The company maintains a very healthy **Current Ratio of 3.91**, indicating it can easily cover its short-term obligations.
*   **Cash Position:** NVIDIA holds **$62.56 billion** in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments, providing a massive war chest for R&D and strategic acquisitions.
*   **Debt Profile:** The **Debt-to-Equity ratio is 7.255** (note: this may be skewed by how liabilities are categorized in the raw data), but total debt of **$11.04 billion** is negligible compared to total stockholders' equity of **$157.29 billion**.
*   **Asset Growth:** Total assets have grown from $111.6 billion to **$206.8 billion** within a year, driven by cash accumulation and increased investments.

### Cash Flow Dynamics
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** NVIDIA is a cash-generating machine, with a TTM FCF of **$58.13 billion**. The most recent quarter alone generated **$34.90 billion** in FCF.
*   **Capital Expenditure:** CapEx remains relatively low (~$1.28 billion in the last quarter) compared to its operating cash flow, highlighting a highly efficient, asset-light business model.
*   **Shareholder Returns:** The company is aggressively returning value through stock repurchases, spending billions quarterly (e.g., $12.46 billion in Oct 2025).

---

## 5. Actionable Insights for Traders

### Bullish Indicators
1.  **Earnings Acceleration:** The gap between the TTM PE (44.88) and Forward PE (19.44) is a strong signal that analysts expect earnings to double or more in the near term.
2.  **Growth vs. Value:** A PEG ratio of 0.68 is exceptionally attractive for a company with this level of market dominance, suggesting the stock is not yet "overpriced" relative to its growth.
3.  **Operational Efficiency:** Operating margins of 65% are nearly unheard of at this scale, providing a massive cushion against potential cost increases.

### Risk Factors
1.  **High Volatility:** A **Beta of 2.24** indicates the stock is more than twice as volatile as the broader market.
2.  **Concentration Risk:** The extreme growth is tied to AI demand. Any slowdown in AI infrastructure spending by "Hyperscalers" (Microsoft, Google, AWS) would hit NVDA hard.
3.  **Valuation Sensitivity:** Despite the PEG ratio, a Price-to-Book of 33.9 suggests the stock is priced for perfection.
