# Fundamental Analysis Report: PayPal Holdings, Inc. (PYPL)
**Date:** May 12, 2026
**Ticker:** PYPL
**Recommendation:** Value-Oriented Buy/Hold

## 1. Company Profile & Basic Financials
PayPal Holdings, Inc. operates in the **Financial Services** sector, specifically within the **Credit Services** industry. As a global leader in digital payments, PayPal provides a two-sided network that enables merchants and consumers to send and receive payments.

**Key Market Metrics:**
*   **Market Capitalization:** ~$40.26 Billion
*   **Current Price Context:** The stock has experienced significant volatility over the last year, with a 52-week range of $38.46 to $79.50. It is currently trading well below its 200-day average ($58.42), suggesting a bearish trend or a potential bottoming process.
*   **Beta:** 1.395 (Higher volatility than the overall market).

## 2. Financial Performance Analysis
### Revenue & Profitability
PayPal has demonstrated steady revenue growth and maintained healthy margins over the past five quarters.
*   **Revenue Trend:** Quarterly revenue has grown from $7.79 billion (March 2025) to $8.35 billion (March 2026). This indicates a resilient top-line growth trajectory despite intense competition in the fintech space.
*   **Margins:** 
    *   **Profit Margin:** 14.99%
    *   **Operating Margin:** 17.97%
    *   **Gross Profit (TTM):** $13.81 Billion
*   **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** TTM EPS stands at $5.33, with a forward projection of $5.80, indicating expected earnings growth.

### Efficiency Ratios
*   **Return on Equity (ROE):** 25.12% — This is a very strong ROE, showing that PayPal is highly efficient at generating profits from shareholders' equity.
*   **Return on Assets (ROA):** 4.74% — Typical for a financial services company with a large balance sheet.

## 3. Financial Position & Solvency
### Balance Sheet Health
PayPal maintains a robust balance sheet with strong liquidity.
*   **Liquidity:** A **Current Ratio of 1.259** indicates that the company can comfortably cover its short-term obligations.
*   **Debt Profile:** 
    *   **Total Debt:** $9.41 Billion (as of March 31, 2026), which is a decrease from $11.42 Billion in March 2025.
    *   **Debt to Equity:** 58.28% — This is a manageable level of leverage for the industry.
*   **Book Value:** $22.02 per share, with a Price-to-Book (P/B) ratio of 2.07, suggesting the stock is not excessively expensive relative to its assets.

## 4. Cash Flow & Shareholder Returns
PayPal is a cash-generating powerhouse, using its liquidity to aggressively return value to shareholders.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** TTM FCF is $4.08 Billion. Quarterly FCF has been consistently positive, reaching $903 Million in the most recent quarter.
*   **Share Repurchases:** The company is executing a massive buyback program, spending approximately **$1.5 Billion per quarter** on repurchasing capital stock.
*   **Share Count Reduction:** This strategy is working; diluted average shares decreased from **999 million (March 2025)** to **920 million (March 2026)**, which directly supports EPS growth.

## 5. Valuation Analysis
The most striking aspect of PayPal's current fundamentals is its valuation.
*   **PE Ratio (TTM):** 8.56
*   **Forward PE:** 7.86
*   **PEG Ratio:** 0.82
A PEG ratio below 1.0 typically indicates that a stock is **undervalued** relative to its growth rate. A PE of ~8.5 is exceptionally low for a company with a 25% ROE and consistent revenue growth, suggesting that the market may be overpricing the risks associated with competition.

## 6. Actionable Insights for Traders
*   **Value Play:** PYPL is currently a classic "value" stock. The disconnect between its strong ROE/FCF and its low PE ratio presents a significant opportunity for long-term investors.
*   **EPS Catalyst:** The aggressive share buyback program acts as a synthetic growth engine. Even if net income remains flat, the shrinking share count will drive EPS upward.
*   **Technical Warning:** While fundamentals are strong, the stock is trading below its 200-day moving average. Traders should look for a trend reversal or a "double bottom" on the chart before entering large positions to avoid "catching a falling knife."
*   **Risk Factor:** Monitor the growth of competing digital wallets. Any further compression in operating margins could negate the valuation advantage.
