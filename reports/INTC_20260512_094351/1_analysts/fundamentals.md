# Fundamental Analysis Report: Intel Corporation (INTC)
**Date:** May 12, 2026
**Ticker:** INTC

## 1. Company Profile & Overview
Intel Corporation is a global leader in the semiconductor industry, operating within the technology sector. The company is currently undergoing a massive strategic pivot towards a "foundry-first" model, aiming to reclaim its manufacturing leadership while continuing to design its own CPUs and GPUs. This transition involves significant capital expenditure to build new fabrication plants (fabs) and adopt advanced process nodes.

## 2. Financial Performance Analysis
### Revenue and Profitability
Intel's revenue has remained relatively stable on a quarterly basis, with the most recent quarter (ending 2026-03-31) reporting **$13.58 billion**. However, the bottom line has been highly volatile:
*   **Gross Profit:** The company maintained a healthy gross profit of **$5.35 billion** in the latest quarter, indicating that its core product pricing remains competitive.
*   **Operating Income:** Operating income was **$934 million**, showing that the core business is still operationally profitable.
*   **Net Income:** Despite operational profits, Intel reported a significant net loss of **-$3.73 billion** for the quarter ending March 31, 2026. This was primarily driven by **$5.23 billion in "Other Income Expense,"** which included substantial write-offs and special charges.
*   **EPS:** The diluted EPS for the latest quarter was **-$0.73**, contributing to a TTM EPS of **-0.6**.

### Investment in Innovation
Intel continues to invest heavily in Research and Development (R&D), spending **$3.38 billion** in the most recent quarter. This is critical for their goal of regaining process leadership.

## 3. Financial Health (Balance Sheet Analysis)
Intel maintains a robust balance sheet that provides a cushion for its aggressive expansion strategy:
*   **Assets vs. Liabilities:** As of March 31, 2026, Intel holds **$205.3 billion in total assets** against **$80.3 billion in total liabilities**.
*   **Equity:** Stockholders' equity stands at **$111.4 billion**, with a tangible book value of **$88.2 billion**.
*   **Liquidity:** The **Current Ratio of 2.31** indicates strong short-term liquidity. The company holds **$32.8 billion** in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments.
*   **Debt Profile:** Total debt is **$45.03 billion**, with a net debt of **$27.78 billion**. While debt is significant, it is well-managed relative to the company's total asset base and equity.

## 4. Cash Flow Analysis
The cash flow statement reveals the true cost of Intel's transition:
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** For the quarter ending March 31, 2026, operating cash flow was **$1.1 billion**, a decrease from the $4.29 billion seen in December 2025.
*   **Capital Expenditure (CapEx):** Intel is spending aggressively, with **$3.64 billion** in CapEx in the last quarter alone.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Consequently, FCF is deeply negative (**-$2.54 billion** for the quarter and **-$8.3 billion TTM**). The company is burning cash to build its future infrastructure.
*   **Investing Activities:** To offset the CapEx burn, Intel has been active in selling investments, generating **$14.4 billion** from the sale of investments in the most recent quarter.

## 5. Key Financial Metrics & Valuation
*   **Market Cap:** ~$620.6 Billion.
*   **Forward P/E:** **80.48**. This is exceptionally high, suggesting that the market is pricing in a massive recovery in earnings (Forward EPS of 1.53).
*   **Price to Book (P/B):** **5.4**, indicating the stock is trading at a premium to its accounting value.
*   **Beta:** **2.19**, highlighting that INTC is significantly more volatile than the broader market.
*   **PEG Ratio:** **1.36**, which suggests that while the P/E is high, the growth expectations are also high.

## 6. Actionable Insights for Traders

### Bull Case (Growth/Recovery)
*   **Foundry Pivot:** If Intel successfully executes its process node roadmap and attracts major external foundry customers, the current CapEx burn will transition into a massive revenue stream.
*   **Valuation Gap:** The gap between TTM EPS (-0.6) and Forward EPS (1.53) shows a projected turnaround. Traders betting on the "comeback" story will find the current transition period a strategic entry point.
*   **Strong Solvency:** The massive equity base and liquidity mean Intel is unlikely to face a solvency crisis despite negative FCF.

### Bear Case (Risk/Caution)
*   **Execution Risk:** The high Forward P/E leaves no room for error. Any delay in fab construction or failure to hit process milestones could lead to a sharp valuation correction.
*   **Cash Burn:** The reliance on selling investments to fund operations is not sustainable indefinitely. Intel must return to positive FCF.
*   **Volatility:** With a Beta of 2.19, the stock is prone to large swings based on macro tech trends and competitive moves from TSMC or NVIDIA.
