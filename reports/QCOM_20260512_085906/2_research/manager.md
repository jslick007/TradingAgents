**Recommendation**: Hold

**Rationale**: The debate over QCOM presents a classic conflict between fundamental paradigm shifts and technical/geopolitical reality. The bull case is compelling, centered on the transition from a mobile-centric company to an 'Ambient AI' powerhouse, supported by a strong PEG ratio of 0.94, elite ROE (36%), and massive Free Cash Flow ($9.59B). However, the bear case highlights critical risks that cannot be ignored: an extremely overbought technical profile (RSI of 88), extreme volatility in net income due to tax adjustments, and the binary geopolitical risk of U.S.-China relations. While the bull's argument regarding 'price discovery' and the additive nature of new revenue streams (Automotive/AR) is strong, the bear's warning about the speculative nature of these unproven revenue drivers and the potential for a violent mean reversion is equally valid. Given the parabolic nature of the recent move and the high-stakes geopolitical environment, the evidence is currently balanced between massive upside potential and significant downside risk.

**Strategic Actions**: Maintain current positions. For existing holders, tighten stop-losses to protect gains against a potential mean-reversion toward the 200 SMA or recent support levels. For new capital, avoid chasing the current parabolic move at these RSI levels; instead, wait for a meaningful technical pullback or a period of consolidation to establish a position. Do not increase exposure until there is clearer evidence of 'Ambient AI' contributing to realized top-line revenue or a stabilization in geopolitical sentiment regarding China.
