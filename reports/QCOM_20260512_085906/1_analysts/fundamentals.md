# Fundamental Analysis Report: QUALCOMM Incorporated (QCOM)
**Date:** May 12, 2026
**Ticker:** QCOM

## 1. Company Profile
QUALCOMM Incorporated operates within the **Technology** sector, specifically the **Semiconductors** industry. As a leader in wireless technology, the company provides a wide array of semiconductor products and software services. With a market capitalization of approximately **$237.86 billion**, QCOM remains a dominant force in the mobile connectivity and chip design space.

## 2. Financial Performance Analysis
### Revenue and Profitability
Qualcomm shows a consistent revenue stream, averaging between $10 billion and $12 billion per quarter over the last year.
*   **Revenue (TTM):** $44.49 billion.
*   **Gross Profit (TTM):** $24.38 billion, maintaining a strong gross margin.
*   **Operating Performance:** The operating margin stands at **22.06%**, reflecting efficient management of operational costs despite significant investments in Research and Development (R&D), which consistently hover around $2.2B to $2.5B per quarter.
*   **Net Income Volatility:** Net income has shown significant quarterly volatility due to tax adjustments. For instance, in Q3 2025 (ending 2025-09-30), the company reported a net loss of **$3.12 billion** primarily driven by a large tax provision of $6.09 billion. Conversely, in Q1 2026 (ending 2026-03-31), a tax benefit of $5.14 billion pushed net income to **$7.37 billion**.

### Earnings Per Share (EPS)
*   **EPS (TTM):** $9.31
*   **Forward EPS:** $10.64 (indicating expected growth in earnings).
*   **Diluted EPS (Latest Quarter):** $6.88 (heavily influenced by the aforementioned tax benefit).

## 3. Financial Health and Solvency
### Balance Sheet Strength
Qualcomm maintains a robust balance sheet with a healthy mix of assets and manageable liabilities.
*   **Liquidity:** The **Current Ratio of 2.37** indicates a strong ability to cover short-term obligations. As of March 31, 2026, current assets ($23.11B) significantly outweigh current liabilities ($9.77B).
*   **Solvency:** The **Debt-to-Equity ratio is 55.98**, which is moderate for the semiconductor industry. Total debt stands at approximately **$15.27 billion**.
*   **Asset Base:** Total assets are **$57.14 billion**, with a significant portion tied to Goodwill and Intangible Assets ($15.83B), reflecting the company's history of strategic acquisitions.

### Efficiency Metrics
*   **Return on Equity (ROE):** An exceptional **36.08%**, demonstrating highly efficient use of shareholder capital to generate profits.
*   **Return on Assets (ROA):** **12.69%**, showing solid profitability relative to total assets.

## 4. Cash Flow Analysis
Qualcomm is a cash-generating powerhouse, which it uses aggressively to reward shareholders.
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** Consistently positive, with the most recent quarter (ending 2026-03-31) bringing in **$2.45 billion**.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** TTM FCF is **$9.59 billion**, providing ample room for dividends, buybacks, and R&D.
*   **Shareholder Returns:**
    *   **Dividends:** The company maintains a dividend yield of **1.55%**, with quarterly payments around $940M - $960M.
    *   **Stock Repurchases:** Qualcomm is very aggressive with buybacks, spending between **$2.4B and $2.8B per quarter** on repurchasing capital stock, which helps support the stock price and increase EPS.

## 5. Valuation Metrics
*   **P/E Ratio (TTM):** 24.24
*   **Forward P/E:** 21.22 (suggesting the stock may become "cheaper" as earnings grow).
*   **PEG Ratio:** **0.94**. A PEG ratio below 1.0 often indicates that a stock is undervalued relative to its expected growth rate.
*   **Price-to-Book (P/B):** 8.76, which is high but typical for high-margin tech companies with significant intangible value.

## 6. Actionable Insights for Traders
*   **Undervaluation Signal:** The **PEG ratio of 0.94** combined with a decreasing Forward P/E suggests that the market may be underestimating QCOM's growth potential. This presents a potential **BUY** opportunity for long-term growth investors.
*   **Volatility Warning:** Traders should be aware that quarterly Net Income is highly susceptible to tax-related swings. Focus on **Operating Income** and **Free Cash Flow** rather than Net Income to gauge the company's actual health.
*   **Supportive Floor:** The aggressive share repurchase program (approx. $10B+ annually) provides a strong psychological and financial floor for the stock price.
*   **Growth Catalyst:** Continued investment in R&D ($2.46B in the last quarter) suggests the company is positioning itself for the next wave of semiconductor innovation (AI, 6G, Automotive).
