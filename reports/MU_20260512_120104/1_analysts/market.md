# Financial Analysis Report: MU (Micron Technology, Inc.)
**Date:** May 12, 2026
**Current Price:** $795.33 (as of May 11, 2026)

## Executive Summary
`MU` is currently experiencing a **parabolic bullish move**. After a period of consolidation and a sharp correction in March 2026, the stock has entered an explosive growth phase, surging from approximately $321 in late March to $795.33 by May 11. While the trend is overwhelmingly positive across all timeframes, the stock is now entering a "severely overextended" zone, characterized by an extremely high RSI and price action trading above the Upper Bollinger Band.

---

## Detailed Technical Analysis

### 1. Trend Analysis (Moving Averages)
The alignment of the moving averages indicates a powerful, synchronized uptrend:
*   **Short-Term (10 EMA):** The price ($795.33) is significantly above the 10 EMA ($641.53). The steep slope of the 10 EMA confirms aggressive short-term buying pressure.
*   **Medium-Term (50 SMA):** The 50 SMA ($456.56) is trending upward, though it is now far below the current price. This gap suggests that the current rally is far faster than the medium-term average growth.
*   **Long-Term (200 SMA):** The 200 SMA ($293.87) confirms a structural long-term bull market. The price is currently trading at ~2.7x its 200-day average, a rarity that typically precedes a significant mean-reversion event.

### 2. Momentum Analysis
*   **RSI (Relative Strength Index):** The RSI stands at **85.84**, which is deep in the **overbought territory** (typically >70). While RSI can remain elevated during strong trends, a reading above 80 often signals that the rally is becoming unsustainable in the immediate term.
*   **MACD & Histogram:** The MACD (78.31) is strongly positive and continuing to rise. The MACD Histogram (26.59) is expanding, indicating that bullish momentum is not just present, but accelerating. There is currently no bearish divergence visible, meaning the trend remains intact.

### 3. Volatility and Range
*   **Bollinger Bands:** The price has broken **above the Upper Bollinger Band ($749.11)**. Trading outside the bands is a sign of extreme momentum; however, it also suggests the price is "stretched" and may soon snap back toward the middle band (20 SMA).
*   **ATR (Average True Range):** ATR has risen to **41.62**, up from ~25 in April. This increase in volatility is expected during a parabolic move but necessitates wider stop-losses to avoid being shaken out by normal price swings.

## Actionable Insights & Trading Strategy

*   **Strategy:** **HOLD / TRAILING STOP.** The trend is too strong to sell blindly, but the risk of a sharp correction is high.
*   **Risk Management:** Implement a trailing stop-loss. A conservative stop could be placed at the **10 EMA (~$641)**. A more aggressive stop could be placed at the recent psychological support of **$750**.

### For New Entrants:
*   **Strategy:** **WAIT FOR PULLBACK.** Entering at $795 carries significant risk due to the extreme distance from the 50 SMA and the overbought RSI.
*   **Entry Zones:** Look for a "buy-the-dip" opportunity if the price retraces to the **$640 - $680 range** (near the 10 EMA) or a deeper correction toward **$550 - $600**.
