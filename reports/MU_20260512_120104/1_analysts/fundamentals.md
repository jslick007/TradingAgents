# Fundamental Analysis Report: Micron Technology, Inc. (MU)
**Date:** 2026-05-12
**Ticker:** MU

## 1. Company Profile & Overview
Micron Technology, Inc. (MU) is a global leader in the semiconductor industry, specializing in the development and manufacture of innovative memory and storage solutions. The company primarily focuses on Dynamic Random Access Memory (DRAM) and NAND Flash memory. Given the current technological landscape, Micron is heavily positioned to benefit from the explosion of Artificial Intelligence (AI), which requires high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and increased storage capacity for data centers.

## 2. Financial Performance Analysis
Micron has experienced an extraordinary surge in financial performance over the last year, reflecting a cyclical upswing in the memory market and the AI-driven demand.

*   **Revenue Growth:** Revenue has grown exponentially. From a quarterly revenue of **$8.05 billion** in February 2025, it skyrocketed to **$23.86 billion** by February 2026. This represents a nearly 3x increase in a single year.
*   **Profitability:**
    *   **Net Income:** Quarterly net income rose from **$1.58 billion** (Feb 2025) to **$13.785 billion** (Feb 2026).
    *   **Operating Margins:** The operating margin has expanded significantly, with the most recent quarterly operating income reaching **$16.135 billion** on $23.86 billion in revenue, indicating immense pricing power and operational efficiency.
    *   **EPS Growth:** Diluted EPS grew from **$1.41** to **$12.07** over the same period.
*   **Margins (TTM):** The company reports a Profit Margin of **41.49%** and an Operating Margin of **67.62%**, which are exceptionally high for the semiconductor hardware sector.

## 3. Financial Health (Balance Sheet)
Micron's balance sheet has strengthened considerably, providing a robust foundation for further expansion.

*   **Asset Growth:** Total assets increased from **$73.05 billion** in February 2025 to **$101.51 billion** in February 2026.
*   **Liquidity:** The current ratio stands at **2.897**, indicating strong short-term liquidity. Cash and cash equivalents have increased to **$13.91 billion**.
*   **Debt Management:** Total debt has been trending downward, from **$14.95 billion** in Feb 2025 to **$10.80 billion** in Feb 2026. The company is actively using its massive cash inflows to reduce leverage.
*   **Equity:** Stockholders' equity grew from **$48.63 billion** to **$72.46 billion**, driven by massive retained earnings from recent profits.

## 4. Cash Flow & Investment Analysis
The cash flow statements reveal a company in a high-growth, high-investment phase.

*   **Operating Cash Flow:** There is a dramatic increase in cash generation, with quarterly operating cash flow jumping from **$3.94 billion** to **$11.90 billion**.
*   **Capital Expenditure (CapEx):** Micron is investing heavily in its future. Quarterly CapEx reached **$6.39 billion** in the most recent quarter. This suggests aggressive builds of new fabrication plants (fabs) and HBM production lines to meet AI demand.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Despite the heavy CapEx, the company generated **$5.52 billion** in FCF in the last quarter, demonstrating that its operational growth is more than funding its expansion.

## 5. Valuation & Market Sentiment
*   **P/E Ratios:** While the TTM PE is **34.66**, the **Forward PE of 7.21** is remarkably low. This suggests that the market expects earnings to continue growing rapidly, making the current price look cheap relative to future profits.
*   **PEG Ratio:** A PEG ratio of **0.26** strongly indicates that the stock is undervalued relative to its earnings growth rate.
*   **Price to Book:** The P/B ratio of **11.42** is high, reflecting the market's premium on the company's intellectual property and future growth potential rather than just its physical assets.
*   **Volatility:** A Beta of **1.92** indicates the stock is nearly twice as volatile as the broader market, typical for a high-growth semiconductor play.

## 6. Actionable Insights for Traders
*   **Bull Case:** The massive divergence between TTM PE (34.66) and Forward PE (7.21) is a strong buy signal for growth investors. The AI tailwind is clearly translating into real bottom-line results. The aggressive CapEx indicates Micron is securing its moat in the HBM market.
*   **Bear Case:** The memory market is historically cyclical. Traders should watch for any signs of oversupply or a slowdown in AI infrastructure spending, which could lead to a sharp correction in pricing.
*   **Technical Note:** The stock is trading well above its 200-day average ($294.02), showing a strong long-term uptrend. However, with a 52-week high of $818.67 and a 50-day average of $456.62, the stock has seen significant volatility.
