# Technical Analysis Report: KBH (as of 2026-05-11)

## Executive Summary
The technical outlook for **KBH** is currently **Strongly Bearish**. The instrument is trading below all key short, medium, and long-term moving averages, with negative momentum confirmed by the MACD and a declining RSI. While the price is approaching the lower Bollinger Band, suggesting a potential short-term exhaustion of the selling pressure, there are no definitive reversal signals to justify a bullish pivot.

---

## Detailed Technical Analysis

### 1. Trend Analysis (Moving Averages)
The trend across all analyzed timeframes is decisively downward:
*   **Short-Term (10 EMA):** The current price of **49.52** is trading below the 10-day EMA (**50.53**). The EMA itself has been sloping downward consistently since early May, acting as a ceiling for any minor rallies.
*   **Medium-Term (50 SMA):** KBH is significantly below its 50-day SMA (**53.20**). The gap between the price and the 50 SMA has widened over the last 30 days, confirming a sustained medium-term downtrend.
*   **Long-Term (200 SMA):** The stock is trading far below the 200-day SMA (**59.02**). This indicates that the long-term structural trend has shifted bearish, and the 200 SMA now serves as a major overhead resistance level.

### 2. Momentum Indicators
*   **MACD:** The MACD is currently at **-1.14**, reflecting strong bearish momentum. The indicator has dropped sharply from positive territory in late April to deep negative values in May, signaling that the sellers are firmly in control.
*   **RSI (Relative Strength Index):** The RSI stands at **40.04**. While this is not yet in the "oversold" territory (typically below 30), the trajectory is downward. The lack of an RSI bounce suggests that the current price decline is not yet exhausted.

### 3. Volatility and Price Range
*   **Bollinger Bands:** The price (**49.52**) is currently trading very close to the **Lower Bollinger Band (47.16)**. Historically, touching or piercing the lower band can signal an oversold condition, but in strong downtrends, prices can "ride the band" lower for extended periods.
*   **ATR (Average True Range):** The ATR is approximately **1.74**, indicating stable volatility. This suggests that the current decline is a steady trend rather than a chaotic crash, allowing for more predictable stop-loss placements.

## Actionable Insights for Traders

*   **Bearish Strategy (Trend Following):** For those following the trend, the current price action confirms a "Sell" or "Short" bias. Any rally toward the **10 EMA (50.53)** or **50 SMA (53.20)** should be viewed as a potential selling opportunity (selling the rip).
*   **Bullish Strategy (Mean Reversion):** Contrarian traders should wait for a confirmed reversal signal before entering. A potential entry trigger would be a bullish MACD crossover accompanied by an RSI dip below 30 and a subsequent bounce off the **Lower Bollinger Band (47.16)**.
*   **Risk Management:** Given the ATR of 1.74, a stop-loss for long positions should be placed at least 2 ATRs below the recent low (approximately below **44.00**) to avoid being stopped out by noise.
