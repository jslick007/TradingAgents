# Fundamental Analysis Report: KB Home (KBH)
**Date:** May 11, 2026
**Ticker:** KBH
**Sector:** Consumer Cyclical | **Industry:** Residential Construction

---

## 1. Executive Summary
KB Home (KBH) is currently presenting as a classic "value play" within the residential construction sector. The company is trading significantly below its book value and maintains a low P/E ratio. While the balance sheet is exceptionally liquid, recent quarterly data shows a sharp decline in revenue and profitability, suggesting either strong seasonality or a cooling in the housing market. The company remains committed to shareholder returns through aggressive share repurchases and a steady dividend.

## 2. Company Profile & Sector Analysis
KB Home operates in the highly cyclical residential construction industry. Its performance is intrinsically linked to mortgage rates, consumer confidence, and housing demand. With a Beta of **1.42**, KBH is more volatile than the broader market, reflecting the sensitivity of the homebuilding sector to macroeconomic shifts.

## 3. Financial Performance Analysis
### Income Statement Trends (Quarterly)
The company has experienced a significant downturn in its most recent reported quarter (ending February 28, 2026):
*   **Revenue Contraction:** Revenue dropped to **$1.077 billion** in Feb 2026, compared to **$1.694 billion** in Nov 2025. This represents a ~36% sequential decline.
*   **Earnings Erosion:** Net income plummeted to **$33.4 million** (EPS $0.52) from a steady range of ~$100M+ (EPS ~$1.50) in previous quarters.
*   **Margin Pressure:** The sharp drop in revenue suggests either a decrease in home deliveries or a significant decrease in the average selling price per unit.

## 4. Financial Position & Solvency
### Balance Sheet Strength
KBH maintains a fortress-like liquidity position:
*   **Liquidity:** A **Current Ratio of 7.80** is remarkably high, indicating that the company has nearly 8 times the current assets needed to cover its short-term liabilities.
*   **Asset Composition:** The balance sheet is dominated by **Inventory (~$5.7 billion)**, which is typical for homebuilders holding land and homes under construction.
*   **Debt Profile:** With a **Debt to Equity ratio of 49.85%**, the company maintains a balanced capital structure. Net debt stands at approximately **$1.69 billion**.
*   **Book Value:** The company's book value per share is **$61.53**, while it is trading at a **Price-to-Book of 0.78**, meaning the market is valuing the company at a 22% discount to its accounting value.

## 5. Cash Flow & Capital Allocation
### Cash Flow Dynamics
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** KBH exhibits extreme seasonality. Operating cash flow was **-$125.4 million** in Feb 2026, following a strong **$303.8 million** in Nov 2025.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** FCF followed the same trend, turning negative in the most recent quarter (**-$138.6 million**), though the TTM FCF remains positive at **$427.3 million**.
*   **Shareholder Returns:** The company is aggressively reducing share count. In the last few quarters, repurchases have ranged from **$50 million to $200 million** per quarter. This, combined with a **2.09% dividend yield**, shows a strong commitment to returning capital to shareholders.

## 6. Valuation Analysis
*   **P/E Ratio (TTM):** **9.24**, which is relatively low for the industry, suggesting the stock is undervalued relative to current earnings.
*   **Forward P/E:** **10.70**, indicating a slight expectation of earnings normalization or a modest decrease in future EPS.
*   **P/B Ratio:** **0.78**, a strong signal for value investors as the stock trades below the liquidation value of its assets.

## 7. Actionable Insights & Recommendations

### For Traders/Investors:
*   **The Value Opportunity:** KBH is fundamentally "cheap." Trading below book value with a single-digit P/E makes it an attractive target for value investors who believe the housing market will stabilize or recover.
*   **The Risk Factor:** The recent collapse in quarterly revenue and net income is a red flag. Traders should monitor mortgage rate trends and new home start data. If the Feb 2026 decline is the start of a broader trend rather than seasonality, the "value" may be a "value trap."
*   **Buyback Support:** The aggressive share repurchase program provides a floor for the stock price and increases the ownership stake of remaining shareholders.

**Recommendation:** **HOLD/ACCUMULATE** on dips. The extreme liquidity and low valuation provide a safety margin, but the recent earnings volatility warrants caution before aggressive buying.
